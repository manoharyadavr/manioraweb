Q // How much does a website cost?

Fixed prices, no hourly fees. Google Business Profile setup is ₹1,999, a 5-page Starter Website is ₹9,999, and a Business Website with WhatsApp ordering or booking is ₹19,999. Bigger projects get a fixed quote upfront.

Q // What if I don't like the design?

You see a free sample before you pay anything. After that, two rounds of changes are included in every website package, so we adjust it until it feels right. Anything beyond that is quoted before I start.

Q // Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and paid by you, and after final payment you own the finished website and its content. If you ever want to move to another developer, you can.

Q // How long does delivery take?

A Starter Website goes live in 7 days, with a preview link by day 3. A Business Website takes about 10 days. The clock starts once I have your logo, photos and details.

Q // How do I pay?

50% advance by UPI or bank transfer before work starts, and 50% before the site goes live. The Care Plan is optional and billed monthly.

Q // Do I own 100% of the code & domain?

Yes. You retain 100% ownership of source code, domains, hosting accounts, and content upon handover.

Q // Can I edit content myself?

Yes. We build simple editor controls so your team can update copy, images, or blog posts without writing code.
