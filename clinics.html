Can patients book appointments directly on the website?

Every package has a WhatsApp appointment button, so patients can message you in one tap. The Business Website also has an appointment request form that sends you the details.

Will my clinic show up on Google?

The site includes basic SEO and links to your Google Business Profile, which helps. I can’t promise a particular ranking, because nobody honestly can.

Do I own the website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and you own the website content and finished files after full payment.

What if I want to change timings or add a doctor later?

Small changes are covered by the Care Plan from ₹999 a month. You can also ask for one-off changes, which are quoted upfront.

Do you share patient data with anyone?

The website doesn’t collect patient health information. Appointment messages go to your own WhatsApp. See the Privacy Policy for details.
