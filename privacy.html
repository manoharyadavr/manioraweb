Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information Maniora collects when you visit this website or contact us, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Maniora is a web development studio based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India, run by Mani. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Maniora" mean that studio.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need:

  • What you send us. Your name, phone number, business details and message when you use the contact form, WhatsApp, a phone call or email. The contact form does not store your message on our server: it opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and you choose whether to send it.
  • Project information you share so we can prepare a sample, quote or website, such as your logo, photos, services and timings.
  • Usage and technical data such as pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location and IP address, collected through analytics tools and our hosting provider.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare samples, quotes and projects.
  • To deliver, support and improve our services and this website.
  • To measure how the website is used and how our marketing performs.
  • To keep the website secure and to meet legal obligations.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Tag Manager, which may load analytics and advertising measurement tools such as Google Analytics or Google Ads. These use cookies or similar technologies to count visits and measure results. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting, analytics and messaging services (including WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta), or when the law requires it. When you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies.

6. Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as needed for the purposes above, to meet legal or accounting requirements, or to resolve disputes. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose, subject to applicable law. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make a material change, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
